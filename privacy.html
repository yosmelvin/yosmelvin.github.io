Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

YMT Solution ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data this website collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Contact form submissions — name, email, phone, message and project type you voluntarily send via our form. Delivered to us through Formspree.
  • Chat messages — if you use the live chat widget (Tawk.to) or contact us on WhatsApp, that service processes your messages under its own privacy policy.
  • Preferences — theme, language and cookie-consent choices stored in your browser's localStorage.
  • Usage analytics — only if you accept cookies and an analytics provider is enabled; currently none is active by default.

Cookies & tracking

  • Strictly necessary — localStorage preferences (theme, language, consent). No cookies are set by this site itself.
  • Third-party — Tawk.to chat and Google Maps may set their own cookies only after you accept via the cookie banner. You can change your choice anytime via "Cookie Settings" in the footer.

Third-party services

  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions.
  • Tawk.to — live chat widget (consent-gated).
  • Google Maps — embedded map on the contact section.
  • WhatsApp / LINE — links that open their apps; they handle data under their own policies.

How we use data

Only to respond to your inquiries, deliver our services, and improve this website. We do not sell your data.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data by emailing ymtsolution@gmail.com.

Layanan pihak ketiga

Cara kami menggunakan data

Hanya untuk menanggapi pertanyaan Anda, memberikan layanan, dan meningkatkan situs web ini. Kami tidak menjual data Anda.
